# Brand asset provenance

Source: [Official Hellyeah brand page](https://www.hellyeahai.com/brand), retrieved October 18, 2026. The original JSON colors, type rules and logo URLs are in `brand-tokens.json`.

The following public assets were copied byte-for-byte for reliable self-hosted rendering:

- `web/assets/lockup-white.svg`: https://www.hellyeahai.com/assets/brand/lockup-white.svg
- `web/assets/lockup-orange.svg`: https://www.hellyeahai.com/assets/brand/lockup-orange.svg
- `web/assets/glyph-orange.svg`: https://www.hellyeahai.com/assets/brand/glyph-orange.svg
- `web/assets/icon-orange.svg`: https://www.hellyeahai.com/assets/brand/icon-orange.svg
- `web/assets/general-sans.woff2`: https://www.hellyeahai.com/_next/static/immutable/media/GeneralSans_Variable-s.p.3dbb_vuk3uz4x.woff2
- `web/assets/lastik.otf`: https://www.hellyeahai.com/_next/static/immutable/media/lastik_regular-s.p.1uvntgusw41_y.otf

Logo geometry and colors are unchanged. Lastik uses only its provided Regular face; no synthetic bold or italic is applied. GeneralSans is constrained to its 200–600 weight range. The orange primary button uses black text. Client creative mockups use a fictional Forma brand and are not claims about any real creative or performance result.

The October 19 homepage rebuild also uses `web/assets/owners.webp`, downloaded unchanged from https://www.hellyeahai.com/assets/hero/owners-v2.webp. It is the public homepage's original owner illustration, displayed over white with its transparency intact. The page follows the public site's bordered frame, light surfaces, orange halo, type hierarchy, and product walkthrough structure. New feature copy describes this interactive prototype; it does not represent an official Hellyeah product release.
